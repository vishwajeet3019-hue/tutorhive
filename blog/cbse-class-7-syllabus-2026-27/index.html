Class 7 syllabus

CBSE Class 7 Syllabus 2026-27: Every Chapter in Maths, Science, Social Science and English

Class 7 is where the middle school years settle into a routine, and also where algebra, integers and congruence start to appear in Maths. CBSE schools follow NCERT's textbooks, and the Class 7 books are new-generation titles first published in 2025, so the chapters look quite different from the books an older sibling or cousin may have used. Maths and Social Science each come in two parts, which makes it easy to miss half of the syllabus. This guide lists every chapter in the four core subjects for 2026-27, using NCERT's own contents pages and descriptions, and points out where children usually need the most help.

The short answer

Class 7 CBSE students use four core NCERT books: Ganita Prakash for Maths in two parts (8 and 7 chapters), Curiosity for Science (12 chapters), Exploring Society: India and Beyond for Social Science in two parts (12 and 8 chapters) and Poorvi for English (5 units). All are reprinted for 2026-27 and differ from older Class 7 books. Class 7 has no board exam, so your school sets the exams and decides the order of chapters. Make sure your child has both parts of Maths and Social Science.

What is different about Class 7 now

  • New books. NCERT's Class 7 Maths Part I and Science were first published in April 2025, and Maths Part II in October 2025. Chapter names and order differ from earlier editions, so older guides, worksheets and notes will not map one-to-one.
  • Two parts for Maths and Social Science. Both parts are part of the syllabus.
  • An activity-based style. NCERT says the Maths books weave storytelling and hands-on activities together and encourage students to find many ways to solve problems, and that the Science book aims for hands-on learning over rote memorisation. Tests in this style often check understanding, as we explain in our guide to competency-based questions.
  • A third language. R3 is compulsory from Class 6 in 2026-27, and it continues into Class 7 in 2027-28. See our three-language policy guide.

You can see the official books on NCERT's textbook page. The chapter lists below come from the contents pages of the 2026-27 editions.

Where children often need extra support: NCERT's own note to teachers suggests giving more time to the ideas that are new to students, naming congruence of triangles and central tendency. Algebra also begins here, in Part I Chapter 4 and Part II Chapter 7, and Class 8 builds on it. Integers (Part II Chapter 2) and fractions (Part I Chapter 8) are other chapters where a gap quickly shows. Our guide to improving your child's maths marks gives a practical plan.

How to plan the year

  1. Get both parts. Make sure your child has Maths Part I and Part II, and Social Science Part 1 and Part 2, and ask when each part will be taught.
  2. Ask for the annual plan. Schools decide the order of chapters and exam dates, so this tells you what is coming.
  3. Revise soon after teaching. A short weekly recall, using the questions at the end of each chapter, prevents the end-of-term pile-up.
  4. Watch the new ideas. Algebra, integers and congruence are where Class 7 gaps often start. If marks slip, read why marks drop in Classes 6 to 8 before adding more hours.
  5. Look ahead. Class 8 builds on Class 7. See our Class 8 syllabus guide and, for the Class 6 books, the Class 6 guide.

Frequently asked questions

Which books does CBSE Class 7 use in 2026-27?

CBSE schools follow NCERT textbooks: Ganita Prakash Part I and Part II for Maths, Curiosity for Science, Exploring Society: India and Beyond Part 1 and Part 2 for Social Science, and Poorvi for English, plus language books chosen by the school.

How many chapters are there in Class 7 Maths?

Class 7 Maths has two books: Part I with 8 chapters and Part II with 7 chapters, so 15 chapters in total. Part I includes Large Numbers Around Us and Expressions using Letter-Numbers, and Part II includes Operations with Integers and Finding the Unknown.

How many chapters are there in Class 7 Science?

NCERT's Curiosity for Grade 7 has 12 chapters, beginning with The Ever-Evolving World of Science and ending with Earth, Moon, and the Sun.

Is the Class 7 syllabus different from earlier years?

Yes. The Class 7 books are new-generation NCERT titles first published in 2025, aligned to the National Curriculum Framework for School Education 2023. Chapter names and order differ from earlier editions.

Is there a board exam in Class 7?

No. CBSE conducts board examinations in Class 10 and Class 12. In Class 7 your school sets the exams and decides the schedule, so ask for the annual plan.

Which Class 7 chapters do children usually find hardest?

It varies by child, but chapters that introduce new ideas tend to need extra practice. In Maths those include congruence, mean, median and mode, which NCERT singles out as new to students, plus the first steps in algebra and operations with integers.
