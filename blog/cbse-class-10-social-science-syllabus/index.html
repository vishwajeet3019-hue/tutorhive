CBSE Class 10 Social Science Syllabus and How to Score Well

Social Science is often the subject parents worry about least and students under-revise most. This guide lays out the four sections of the CBSE Class 10 Social Science syllabus and where marks are typically won or lost in each.

History

Covers the rise of nationalism in Europe and India, the making of a global economy, industrialisation, and the growth of print culture. Chapters are narrative and date-heavy, and questions often ask students to explain causes and effects rather than just recall events — answers that only list facts without connecting them tend to lose marks.

Geography

Covers resources and development, agriculture, water resources, minerals and energy, manufacturing industries, and the country's transport and trade networks. This section leans on diagrams, maps, and classification (types of resources, types of farming, types of industries), which makes organised revision notes especially useful here.

Political Science (Civics)

Covers power sharing, federalism, political parties, and the working and outcomes of democracy in India. Many answers in this section are conceptual and example-based, so students who can support a definition with a real example usually score noticeably higher than those who state the definition alone.

Economics

Covers economic development, sectors of the Indian economy, money and credit, globalisation, and consumer rights. This section has the most numerical and diagram-based content of the four (loan calculations, sector data, comparisons), so it benefits from the same worked-example practice as a maths chapter, not just reading.

Where marks are actually lost

Across all four sections, the gap between "knowing the chapter" and "scoring well" usually comes down to three habits: structuring long answers into clear, labelled points instead of one dense paragraph; using the specific terms from the textbook rather than a rough paraphrase; and not skipping map-based questions in History and Geography, which are one of the most reliably scorable parts of the paper once practised.

Because the exact chapter list within each section is periodically revised by CBSE, always confirm the current syllabus against the official CBSE circular or your child's school before finalising a study plan.

Frequently asked questions

What are the four sections of CBSE Class 10 Social Science?

CBSE Class 10 Social Science is divided into History, Geography, Political Science (Civics), and Economics, plus a Disaster Management or map-work component in some years. The exact chapter list within each section is revised periodically by CBSE, so always confirm against the current year's official circular.

Why do students lose marks in Social Science despite knowing the content?

Most marks are lost to answer-writing habits rather than missing knowledge: not structuring long answers around clear points, skipping map-based questions out of unfamiliarity, or writing vague answers that don't use the specific terms examiners look for. Fixing this needs practice writing full answers, not just reading the chapters.

How important is map work in CBSE Class 10 Social Science?

Map-based questions in History and Geography are a reliably scorable section if practised regularly, but are often skipped by students who assume they're too difficult or unpredictable. Regular practice locating and marking the specified locations turns this into one of the easiest sections to score full marks in.

How should a student revise for Social Science before boards?

A useful approach is separate revision passes for facts and dates, key terms and definitions, map locations, and full-length written answers, rather than only re-reading the textbook. Previous years' papers help identify which answer formats (short, long, source-based, map) still need practice closer to the exam.
